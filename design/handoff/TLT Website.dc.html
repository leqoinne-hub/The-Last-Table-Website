OUR STORY

One light still on.

Every great Chicago night has a last table. It's the one the captain holds for the regulars, the one still laughing when the chairs go up.

PHOTO · THE ROOM FROM THE BAR, BRASS AND MARBLE

We built a room for that table at State & Erie: forest-green leather and cognac, blackened millwork, black marble and brass, lit low enough that the city outside goes quiet. Dinner runs into cocktails, cocktails run into a live set, and nobody's checking the time.

The idea is simple. The last seating of the night gets the same care as the first — the same pour, the same plate, the same welcome at one in the morning as at four in the afternoon.

The Last Table is part of L&A Core Hospitality, a Chicago group that believes hospitality is a craft, not a performance. Pull up a chair. Stay for one more.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

House notes.

A few things worth knowing before your first night with us.

Dress code+

Dress for a night out. Collared shirts, dark denim and evening wear all belong here. Please leave athletic wear, ball caps and beachwear at home.

Age policy+

Guests of every age are welcome for dinner and Sunday brunch with an adult. After 10 PM the room is 21 and over, with valid ID.

Reservations & cancellations+

Book on Resy up to 30 days ahead. We hold tables for 15 minutes. Please cancel at least 24 hours ahead; late cancellations and no-shows may be charged $25 per guest.

Larger parties+

Online booking is for up to six guests. For seven or more, send a private dining inquiry and our events team will take it from there.

Walk-ins+

The bar is held for walk-ins every night, first come, first served.

Allergies & dietary needs+

Tell your captain about any allergies when you sit down. Many dishes can be adjusted, and the spirit-free list is always available.

Corkage+

You are welcome to bring a bottle that isn't on our list. Corkage is $50 per bottle, up to two bottles per table.

Celebrations+

Celebrating something? Add it to your Resy notes and we'll take care of the rest.

Live music+

The band starts when dinner winds down, most nights from 10 PM. No cover for dinner guests.

Photos+

Photograph your table, gladly. Please keep the flash off and give the band and the guests around you their night.

Parking & transit+

Valet details are coming soon. The nearest L stop is Grand on the Red Line, two blocks south.

Gift cards+

Sold online through Toast and at the host stand. They are good for dinner, drinks and the late set.

Accessibility+

Tell us what would make your visit easier when you book, or write to info@atthelasttable.com. We want this website to work for everyone; if something is hard to use, email us and we'll fix it.
